The desk advantage

VaR utilization climbs from 70% to 90% over three days. Factor concentration is spiking in rates. The signal fires: reduce your most-concentrated exposure before the forced-selling cascade arrives. That's not just alpha. It's seeing the stress event 2-3 days before it hits and having time to reposition.

  • Same methodology, daily proprietary inputs from SecDB

The theory was proved with quarterly proxies. The internal version uses the real measurement: daily, correctly-signed, desk-level. That gap is the product's moat. With access to VaR data, the internal version gets even stronger.

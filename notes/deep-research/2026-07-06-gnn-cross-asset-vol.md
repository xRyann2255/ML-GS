# Deep Research: Graph Neural Networks for Cross-Asset Volatility Forecasting

**Question:** How are GNNs used to model cross-asset spillovers for RV/IV forecasting, and what are the documented patterns for feeding GNN outputs (embeddings, edge weights, attention) into downstream GBMs/LSTMs?

**Date:** 2026-07-06-gnn-cross-asset-vol · **Sources:** 25 kept of 56 harvested · **Lens:** state of the art, 2025-2026

## Direct Answer

The state of the art for GNN volatility forecasting under a QLIKE/DM/MCS standard is still exactly one lineage: **GNNHAR** (Zhang, Pu, Cucuringu & Dong, Int. J. Forecasting 41(1):377-397, 2025, arxiv.org/abs/2308.01419) — a one-hop GNN on a GLASSO spillover graph over 27 DJIA stocks, trained with **QLIKE loss**, delivering ~13% MSE / ~4% QLIKE over rolling HAR-OLS at 1-day (Table 1, IJF p.385), ~8.7% QLIKE at 1-week, fading to insignificance at 1-month; multi-hop message passing adds nothing (DM significant for 1 of 27 stocks). Every other ST-GNN entry in the 2025-26 frontier — DCRNN-HAR (J. Forecasting 2026), GSP-HAR, ASTGCN, EMGNN — reports MSE/MAE only, never QLIKE, so their headline gains (up to -55% MSE vs naive HAR) remain unproven under our primary loss. On the central hybrid question, the literature does **not** overturn our prior blending verdict: no finance paper demonstrates GNN-embedding-into-GBM beating prediction blending; the nearest documented pipeline (Choi & Kim 2024: 1024-d graph embeddings, UMAP to 32-d, into XGBoost/LightGBM/CatBoost) yields statistically significant but economically tiny gains and has no leakage protocol, while the only rigorous joint GBM+GNN mechanism (BGNN, ICLR 2021, non-finance) argues end-to-end beats two-stage. Graph construction is genuinely contested — GLASSO beats identity adjacency in GNNHAR, but a dumb fully-connected graph beats GLASSO in GNAR-HARX (10-index universe), and graph look-ahead turns out to be a second-order leakage sin (arXiv 2605.23959, Table 1). Confidence: **high** on the GNNHAR numbers and the QLIKE-training lever; **medium** on transferability — the single biggest caveat is that all rigorous results are cross-stock (DJIA) or cross-equity-index, **not cross-asset-class**, on universes and frequencies that differ from our 34-symbol project universe.

## Key Insights

- **The honest upper bound on GNN gains in a rigorous vol setting: ~4% QLIKE at h=1, ~8.7% at h=5, gone by h=22.** GNNHAR1L trained under QLIKE vs HAR-OLS ratios: 0.867 MSE / 0.961 QLIKE (1-day), 0.855 / 0.913 (1-week, best), 1.179 / 0.965 (1-month); GNNHAR1LQ and GNNHAR2LQ retained in the 5% MCS under QLIKE at h=1 (Zhang et al. 2025, Table 1, IJF p.385 / arXiv p.15, arxiv.org/abs/2308.01419). [peer-reviewed]
- **The loss function is a bigger lever than the architecture.** QLIKE-trained variants beat their MSE-trained twins on QLIKE at all horizons and on MSE at h=1/h=5; in top-decile SPY-RV turbulence GNNHAR1LQ has ~13% lower MSE and ~2% lower QLIKE than GNNHAR1LM (Table 2 Panel B, IJF p.386). **Scope correction our earlier note lacked:** at h=22 QLIKE-trained models have 30-50% *worse* MSE than MSE-trained twins (GNNHAR2LM 1.134 vs 2LQ 1.736; 3LM 1.046 vs 3LQ 1.502, Table 1) — "QLIKE training always wins" holds only for h≤5 and for the QLIKE metric. [peer-reviewed]
- **Multi-hop spillovers are dead weight.** GNNHAR2L vs 1L: DM-significant for only AXP (1/27 stocks), cross-sectional p≈0.75 (Fig. 6a, IJF p.388); 3-layer GNN degrades outright (MSE ratio 1.210 at h=1) via over-smoothing diagnosed with mean-average-distance; the GLASSO DJIA graph has diameter 3 (Table A.2, arXiv p.34). One nonlinear hop is the optimum. [peer-reviewed]
- **Linear graph aggregation already captures roughly half the gain.** GHAR (no neural net) scores 0.927 MSE / 0.983 QLIKE at h=1 (Zhang et al. 2025, Table 1); and on realized *covariances* GHAR(GL,Le) gives -2.5% Frobenius / -1.8% QLIKE vs HAR-DRD with MCS p=1.000 (Zhang et al., JFEC nbae026, Table 4 — caveat: QLIKE ratio 1.001 at 1-month, the paper's own "one exception"). [peer-reviewed]
- **The Diebold-Yilmaz-to-GNN bridge is now peer-reviewed but QLIKE-free.** DCRNN-HAR uses the transposed DY generalized-FEVD spillover matrix as adjacency, made dynamic via trading-day masks (Ã_t = E^A_t ⊙ A, §3.2-3.3); best in 48/48 scenarios, 75%-MCS inclusion 48/48 vs HAR's 12/48 (Table 8); SPX h=1 MSE 0.125 vs 0.136 (Chi, Gao & Wang, J. Forecasting 45(3):1311-1324, 2026, arxiv.org/abs/2409.15320). But no QLIKE, no DM, and vs the nearest prior model (STG-Spillover) the margin is only ~4-11%, not the ~55%-vs-naive-HAR headline. [peer-reviewed]
- **Graph construction is contested, and elaborate graphs can lose to dumb ones.** GNNHAR: GLASSO beats identity/no-graph (GHAR 1.7% lower QLIKE than HAR). GNAR-HARX: fully-connected networks *consistently outrank GLASSO* on both QLIKE (-8.5891 FC vs -8.5831 HARX+IV vs -8.5785 HAR) and MSE, with GLASSO instability (erratic edge counts and Jaccard from 2016) diagnosed as the cause (Ó Nualláin 2025, Table 4 p.22, quote p.24, arxiv.org/abs/2510.24443 — MSc thesis supervised by GNAR originator Guy Nason; 10-index universe). EMGNN: fully *learned, evolving* adjacency beats learned-static MTGNN on BTC 1-day vol (rel. MSFE 0.6527 vs 0.7809, MCS p=1.000, Table 5; Zhou et al., Financial Innovation 2025, link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40854-025-00768-x). Likely universe-size-dependent. [peer-reviewed, mixed venues]
- **The embedding-into-GBM pipeline exists but its documented payoff is small.** Choi & Kim (2024, Entropy/PMC10814172): MI + transfer-entropy networks over ETFs → Role2vec/FEATHER 1024-d embeddings → UMAP to 32-d → XGBoost/LightGBM/CatBoost; accuracy gains like XLE 0.5238→0.5651 (t=103.6) are significant but economically modest (Table 8), embeddings are graph-algorithmic (not trained-GNN), and **no leakage protocol for network/embedding construction is described** — a negative exemplar. The only rigorous tree+GNN training mechanism is BGNN (Ivanov & Prokhorenkova, ICLR 2021, arxiv.org/abs/2101.08543): fit new trees to the GNN's gradient updates; end-to-end beats two-stage Res-GNN by 4-14% RMSE (Table 2, §4.1.2) — non-finance, and architecturally the *reverse* (GBDT feeds GNN) of the extract-and-freeze pattern. Net: our 2026-05-12 prediction-blending verdict stands. [peer-reviewed, non-finance for BGNN]
- **Graph look-ahead is a second-order leakage sin.** In the one-switch leakage benchmark, full-sample ("future-informed") graph estimation inflates Sharpe@5bps by ~0.00-0.06 for Ridge/LightGBM/Graph-MLP and 0.13-0.41 for LSTM/Transformer, vs 4.26-26.16 for centered temporal features and same-day execution (Zhang, Li, Peng & Chen 2026, Table 1 p.6, arxiv.org/abs/2605.23959; return-prediction task, not vol). Still estimate graphs on training windows only — GNNHAR's monthly re-estimation on rolling 1000-day windows is the clean template — but prioritize temporal-feature and execution hygiene. [preprint]
- **Cross-asset terms are not a free lunch, and forecast accuracy does not buy portfolio value.** HARX-ElasticNet on 6 futures: RMSE identical to HAR to 4 decimals per-asset, only 7/90 cross-market coefficients nonzero (arXiv 2601.03146, Table 3 p.17 — caveat: 30-day-smoothed Yang-Zhang vol plausibly manufactures the null). Wade (2026, arxiv.org/abs/2605.19278): best-MSE, best-RankIC, and best-Sharpe models are three *different* models; GNN-Sector+Macro Sharpe 0.984 vs HAR per-stock 0.635 while the MSE gap is ~2% (Tables 3/5) — no QLIKE/DM/MCS, survivorship-biased, weekly RV. [preprint]

## What's New Since Our Last Sweep (2026-05-31)

- **Cartea, Cucuringu & Fang (2026), "Network-Augmented Volatility Forecasting with Idiosyncratic Jump Spillovers" (SSRN 6333798)** — the newest entry in the GNNHAR lineage (Cucuringu is the sole common author). Jump variation, useless univariately, cuts 1-day MSE ~30% vs HAR when modelled as an *idiosyncratic* (factor-residual) network spillover; raw/market-based networks add little (abstract, verbatim in OMI Newsletter Vol.5 No.6, March 2026, p.3-4). Abstract-only, no QLIKE visible, PDF paywalled. The graph-construction lesson — build edges from factor residuals, not raw correlations — is new to the repo. [preprint]
- **Boetti & Nunes (2026), GNHAR (arXiv 2606.03828, submitted June 2 — post-dates our sweep)** — directed Granger-causality/connectedness graphs plus option-implied variances as node regressors; MAFE -38% at h=1 vs JC-HAR (Table 2 p.14). Linear GNAR framework, *not* a GNN, evaluated on MAFE/MSFE not QLIKE; explicitly positioned as evidence that linear network models can match GNN complexity. [preprint]
- **DCRNN-HAR upgraded from preprint to peer-reviewed** (J. Forecasting 45(3):1311-1324, April 2026, DOI 10.1002/for.70081). The QLIKE gap we flagged remains: MSE/MAE + 75%-MCS only.
- **Full GNNHAR PDF acquired and verified** (repo previously held headline numbers only): the h=22 MSE penalty of QLIKE training, the over-smoothing diagnosis, the turbulent-regime split, and the graph-diameter-3 detail are all new precision.
- **Gap-fills on the hybrid question** (flagged NOT-covered last sweep, now covered): BGNN for end-to-end GBM+GNN training; Choi & Kim for concrete embedding dimensionality (1024→32) into GBMs and the leakage-protocol gap; EMGNN for learned dynamic adjacency; STID (CIKM 2022, arxiv.org/abs/2208.05233) for the deflation result that per-node identity embeddings + MLP beat STGNNs on standard benchmarks — "what graphs buy can often be bought by node IDs"; the leakage benchmark quantifying graph look-ahead; ASTGCN (J. Empirical Finance 83, 2025, art. 101639) as the peer-reviewed representative of attention-as-regime-dependent-spillover-features (abstract-only: attention interrelations differ in tranquil vs turmoil periods).

## Contradictions & Open Threads

- **GLASSO vs fully-connected vs learned adjacency.** GNNHAR: GLASSO graph beats no-graph/identity (GHAR -1.7% QLIKE vs HAR; Table 1). GNAR-HARX: FC beats GLASSO on QLIKE and MSE outright, with GLASSO edge-instability diagnosed (Table 4 p.22, p.24). EMGNN: learned evolving beats learned static (Table 5) — but crypto, MSFE, no predefined-vs-learned ablation. Plausible reconciliation: 27-stock single-class universes reward sparsification; 10 highly-correlated indices don't. **Experiment on our data:** GHAR-style linear aggregation on the 34-symbol universe with identity vs FC vs GLASSO vs rolling-DY adjacency, h=1/5/22, QLIKE with DM vs the HAR-WLS baseline and MCS over the four graphs. Cheap (all linear, OLS-estimable) and it resolves the single most contested design choice before any GNN is built.
- **Does any ST-GNN win survive QLIKE?** DCRNN-HAR (48/48 on MSE/MAE) and GSP-HAR (13-14/24) never compute QLIKE — the repo's signature trap (MSE wins that vanish under QLIKE). Both repos are open and forecast files are reproducible. **Experiment:** regenerate their SPX/global-index forecasts, score under Patton-robust QLIKE, DM vs rolling HAR. Prediction from the GNNHAR pattern: the h=1 gains shrink to low single digits; the h=22 MSE blowout (-54%) inverts or vanishes.
- **Horizon direction conflict.** GNNHAR gains fade by h=22 (MSE ratio 1.179); DCRNN-HAR MSE gains *grow* with horizon (-13% h=1 to -54% h=22). Different universes (stocks vs global indices), different targets (RV vs sqrt-RV scaled), different metric discipline. Unresolved; the QLIKE re-scoring above is the cleanest arbiter.
- **Embedding features vs prediction blending for the GBM stage.** No finance paper tests trained-GNN embeddings in LightGBM against simply blending the GNN's scalar forecast. Choi-Kim (non-GNN embeddings, tiny gains, no leakage control) and BGNN (joint beats two-stage, non-finance, reverse direction) bracket the question without answering it. **Experiment:** GNNHAR1LQ on our universe; arm A = LightGBM(HAR features + GNN scalar forecast), arm B = LightGBM(HAR features + final-layer node embedding, 8/16/32-d), purged k-fold with embargo, QLIKE + DM. Our prior stands until arm B wins: expect A ≈ B at ~zero extra complexity for A.
- **Cartea-Cucuringu-Fang QLIKE unknown.** The 30% MSE headline (abstract) is the largest claimed network gain in the lineage, but MSE-only claims in this exact literature have repeatedly failed to transfer. Need the full SSRN PDF; until then, treat as promising-unverified and adopt only the design idea (factor-residual networks for edge construction).
- **Attention-as-spillover-features has no open numbers.** ASTGCN (JEF 2025) is the peer-reviewed anchor for GAT-attention interpretability but is abstract-only. The interpretable-edge-weight agenda currently rests on qualitative claims; do not cite deltas.

## New vs Already Known

The repo already had the core verdict (graphs win with significance on realized covariance, not univariate daily QLIKE — GHAR, JFEC), the GNNHAR headline findings (one-hop only, QLIKE-loss training, from notes/features/cross-asset.md), the DCRNN-HAR no-QLIKE caveat, and the LSTM→LightGBM blending-beats-stacking guidance. New this run: (1) the full GNNHAR PDF with the verified Table 1 grid and a **correction** — QLIKE training's superiority is scoped to h≤5 and to the QLIKE metric, with 30-50% MSE penalties at h=22; (2) DCRNN-HAR's peer-review upgrade plus the deflating ~4-11%-vs-STG-Spillover attribution; (3) three genuinely new 2026 entries — Cartea-Cucuringu-Fang idiosyncratic jump-spillover networks (30% MSE, abstract-only), Boetti-Nunes directed-Granger GNHAR, and the one-switch leakage benchmark showing graph look-ahead is second-order; (4) the graph-construction contradiction (FC beats GLASSO in GNAR-HARX) now documented with numbers; (5) the hybrid-pipeline gap filled: Choi-Kim's 1024→32-d embedding→GBM pipeline (small gains, no leakage protocol), BGNN's joint-training mechanism, EMGNN's learned dynamic adjacency, STID's identity-embedding deflation — collectively confirming, not overturning, the prediction-blending recommendation; and (6) 14 PDFs landed in reference/project-papers/, closing most of the flagged not-yet-covered list except implied-vol/vol-surface GNNs, GNNExplainer-in-finance, and temporal-GNN families (EvolveGCN/DySAT/TGAT) applied to vol, for which no credible vol-specific sources surfaced.

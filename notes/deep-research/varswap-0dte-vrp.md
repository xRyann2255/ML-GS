## Direct Answer

**Thread 1 (replication theory) is fully settled and the canonical chain is short.** The fair variance-swap strike is the price of a SHORT log contract, decomposed by the Breeden-Litzenberger / Carr-Madan spanning identity into a static `1/K^2`-weighted strip of OTM puts and calls plus a dynamic `1/S` futures position — the exact structure GSVIVS01 trades. The primary source is **Demeterfi-Derman-Kamal-Zou (DDKZ) 1999** (GS Quant Strategies note; Eq 26 closed-form strike, Eq 20 path-by-path identity, Eq 42 jump correction), with **Carr-Lee (2009)** the cleanest peer-reviewed modern derivation including the discrete/jump correction, and **Aschakulporn-Zhang (2019)** + **Du-Kapadia (2012)** formalizing the skew/kurtosis premium and the jump bias of the VIX-style strip. The two load-bearing caveats your chapter must carry: (a) the fair strike exceeds ATM implied vol because the `1/K^2` weighting over-weights OTM puts where the equity skew lives — a real, signable premium (DDKZ Eq 31/33), and (b) the replication is exact only for continuous paths; **the leading error is THIRD order in the daily return** and is signed by the risk-neutral mean of cubed returns, so down-jumps make the standard scheme UNDERprice the swap (Carr-Lee 2009 Eq 9). This jump bias is exactly the regime 0DTE lives in.

**Thread 2 (0DTE VRP harvesting + drawdown timing) is an active 2024-2026 frontier and the verdict is nuanced, not bullish.** The current state of the art says: (i) the 0DTE variance risk premium is real and per-unit-time RICHER than the 30-day VRP — annualized intraday VRP runs ~2.87-8.00% vs the ~2% one-month benchmark, "up to four times larger" at the high end (Almeida-Freire-Hizmeri 2024); but (ii) **the single best empirical source on actually harvesting it (Vilkov 2024, "0DTE Trading Rules") concludes the same-day VRP is "small and difficult to monetize after realistic frictions," with net PnL "wide, state-dependent, and dominated by tail risk rather than stable mean carry"** — and that disciplined CONDITIONAL timing (e.g. a 10:00 ET entry rule) is what delivers economically meaningful net performance, not an unconditional short. The most rigorous backtest of the exact overlay GSVIVS01 wants (sell less vol when a vol forecast says risk is high) is **Yang (2024)**: an inverse-volatility / VRP-conditioned overlay on variance swaps raises Sharpe 1.54→1.76 and cuts max drawdown, skew, and kurtosis. **Confidence: high on Thread 1; medium on Thread 2** — the 0DTE empirics are all working papers, the headline Sharpes sit on different assets/samples than ours, and Vilkov's specific monetizability numbers remain gated. **Single biggest caveat:** none of these studies is GSVIVS01 itself; the 0DTE-VRP-drawdown and timing evidence is on related short-vol assets (1-month variance swaps, VIX futures, generic 0DTE straddles), so the bps/Sharpe deltas are directional analogues, not transferable point estimates — they must be re-confirmed on our index.

## Key Insights

- **The fair variance-swap strike IS a short log contract = `1/K^2` OTM strip + dynamic `1/S`.** DDKZ Eq 20 gives the path-by-path identity `V = (2/T)[∫ dS_t/S_t − log(S_T/S_0)]` with the verbatim claim "no expectations or averages have been taken … variance can be captured no matter which path the stock price takes, as long as it moves continuously"; Eq 25 decomposes `−log(S_T/S*)` into short forwards plus a `1/K^2` strip of OTM puts and calls; Eq 26 is the closed-form strike (Demeterfi-Derman-Kamal-Zou 1999, Eq 20 p.17 / Eq 25 p.18 / Eq 26 p.19, emanuelderman.com/.../gs-volatility_swaps.pdf) [practitioner]. This is the exact structure of the GSVIVS01 strip in `GSVIVS01.md §1.2/§2.3`.

- **The `1/K^2` weight is forced by demanding constant variance-vega.** Solving `2ρ + K·dρ/dK = 0` gives `ρ = const/K^2` — the only weighting whose strip has variance-exposure independent of spot (DDKZ 1999, Appendix A Eq A3 p.38, same PDF) [practitioner]. This is the first-principles justification of the `q_i = w·ΔK_i/K_i^2` quantities the strategy actually books (GSVIVS01.md §2.3).

- **Why the strike exceeds ATM IV (the skew premium), quantified.** For skew linear in strike, `K_var ≈ Σ_0^2(1 + 3·T·b^2)`; for skew linear in delta, `K_var ≈ Σ_0^2(1 + (1/√π)·b·√T + (1/12)·b^2/Σ_0^2)` — verbatim "the skew increases the value of the fair variance above the at-the-money-forward level of volatility" (DDKZ 1999, Eq 31 p.23 / Eq 33 p.25) [practitioner]. The worked example: a 1-vol-pt-per-5-strike skew lifts `K_var` from `(20)^2=400` to `(20.467)^2` (DDKZ Table 1 total cost 419.8671 p.21-22). This grounds the "1-3 vol pts skew premium" claim and the "use the swap strike, not ATM IV" correction in GSVIVS01.md §4.3.

- **ATM IV ≈ volatility-swap rate < variance-swap strike — the ordering is empirical, not just theoretical.** Across all indices: "the average value of the realized variance is the lowest, and the variance swap rate is the highest, with the volatility swap rate in the middle"; the var-vs-vol-swap gap is a Jensen/vol-of-vol convexity effect; Carr-Lee (2003) show ATM Black-Scholes IV is an accurate proxy for the vol-swap rate (Carr-Wu 2009, Table 4 discussion + eq.(15) text, engineering.nyu.edu/.../CarrReviewofFinStudiesMarch2009-a.pdf) [peer-reviewed]. Confirms that using ATM IV systematically UNDERstates what GSVIVS01 sells.

- **The replication error is THIRD order in the daily return and signed by E[R^3] — the jump correction that matters at 0DTE.** "The leading term in the replication error is third order in the daily return" and "the standard pricing scheme underprices the variance swap rate when the risk-neutral mean of cubed returns is negative" (Carr-Lee 2009, Eq 9 + prose p.328-329, engineering.nyu.edu/.../annurev.financial.050808.114304.pdf) [peer-reviewed]. DDKZ make this concrete: a downward jump `J` gives a short-swap P&L whose leading term is cubic `(2/3)(J^3/T)`, the quadratic term cancels, and a 15%-down jump scores +101.5 (3-month) to the short (DDKZ 1999, Eq 42 p.31 + Table 5 p.32) [practitioner]. **This is the mechanical source of GSVIVS01's asymmetric tail: down-gap days, not slow grinds, are where the short blows up.**

- **The VIX/`1/K^2` strip is a BIASED measure of true quadratic variation once the price can jump — and the bias bites exactly when jumps dominate.** The VIX-style integrated-variance strip captures the continuous component only; the Bakshi-Kapadia-Madan risk-neutral variance is more accurate under jumps, and their difference IS the jump/tail contribution (the JTIX). The bias is regime-specific: VIX-style `√IV` reads 19% vs a true 20% only when jumps exceed ~70% of total variance; below ~20% jump share the error is <1% (Du-Kapadia 2012, §3.4 eq.28 + Table 1 Panel B p.12, people.umass.edu/nkapadia/docs/Du_Kapadia_August2012.pdf) [preprint]. The 0DTE horizon is precisely the high-jump-share regime, so the chapter should flag that GSVIVS01's strike is a continuous-variance object being sold against a jump-laden realization.

- **Discretization vs truncation vs jump errors are separable and quantified.** On a dense, liquid grid discretization is modest (continuous-price error <0.03% vs trapezoidal 0.20% on 78 SPX strikes); Derman's piecewise-LINEAR wing approximation is the main weakness; truncating the strike range turns it into a corridor swap with large error (37.18 vs 40.00 = 2.82 vol-pt error on [60,140] at 40% vol); and "the effect of jumps on the replication of the variance swap is approximately cubic in the jump size," with the variance-swap replication UNDER-estimating the Bates value by up to ~18% under extreme negative jumps — contra Carr-Wu's "negligible" verdict for ONE-YEAR swaps (Le Floc'h 2018, Tables 8-9 + wing critique, mdpi-res.com/.../jrfm-11-00011-v2.pdf) [peer-reviewed, JRFM]. Note: Le Floc'h's "not negligible" is for 1-year swaps; for 0DTE the relevant jump channel is DDKZ Eq 42 / Carr-Lee Eq 9 directly.

- **The Carr-Madan spanning formula generalizes the strip and gives the explicit skew/kurtosis decomposition.** Any twice-differentiable payoff is spanned by OTM options weighted by `H_xx(K)`; the model-free implied variance decomposes as `VIX = 100·√(σ^2 + (2/τ)·ln[1 + (λ_1/3!)(σ√τ)^3 + (λ_2/4!)(σ√τ)^4])` — i.e. ATM variance PLUS skewness (λ_1) and kurtosis (λ_2) loadings, the option-strip analogue of the Derman skew premium; to bound risk-neutral skew error <1e-3 you need a strike range ≥ [¾F, 4⁄3 F] and step ≤0.1% of forward (Aschakulporn-Zhang 2019, eq.10/eq.20 p.6-9 + abstract error bound, acfr.aut.ac.nz/.../Pakorn-Bakshi,-Kapadia,-and-Madan-2003...pdf) [preprint]. Cite the original Bakshi-Kapadia-Madan (2003, RFS) and Carr-Madan (2001) as the true primaries; this is a re-derivation, not BKM itself.

- **The 0DTE VRP is real and richer per unit time than the 30-day VRP.** Annualized average intraday VRP runs 2.87%-8.00% across time-of-day vs the ~2% one-month benchmark — "up to four times larger than what is usually observed for the one-month horizon" (high end = 8.00% ≈ 4×2%); ATM delta-hedged calls and straddles have significantly negative average returns, so the SELLER earns the premium; 0DTEs largely violate stochastic-dominance bounds with Sharpe ~0.2-0.3 net of worst-case costs (Almeida-Freire-Hizmeri 2024, Intro p.3 + Table 1 + Table 7, paris-december.eu/.../5431_gustavobfreire_2024_complete.pdf) [preprint]. Twist relevant to a drawdown chapter: intraday UPSIDE ("good") VRP is positive and often larger than downside — opposite of the one-month sign pattern.

- **Same-day VRP is hard to monetize after frictions; net PnL is tail-dominated, and CONDITIONAL timing is what works.** Verbatim abstract: "a positive 0DTE variance risk premium exists, but at same-day horizons its economic magnitude is small and difficult to monetize after realistic frictions … disciplined 10:00 ET conditional rules deliver economically meaningful net performance for selected strategies, pointing to selective timing opportunities rather than to a broad unconditional edge"; "net PNL distributions are wide, state-dependent, and dominated by tail risk rather than by stable mean carry" (Vilkov 2024, "0DTE Trading Rules," abstract; SSRN 4641356; sample Sep 2016–early 2026) [preprint, abstract-only — quantitative tables gated]. **This is the single most on-thesis source for GSVIVS01's drawdown-timing question** and it directly supports the project's signal-conditioned-overlay thesis over an always-on short.

- **A vol-forecast-conditioned overlay reduces short-variance drawdowns — the canonical recent backtest.** A Moreira-Muir-style overlay that sells LESS variance when volatility is high raises Sharpe across short-vol assets and cuts the tail: variance swaps 1.54→1.76, VIX futures 0.61→0.78, straddles-with-margin 0.51→0.62, each with reduced negative skewness, kurtosis, and max drawdown; it works because a vol spike predicts a statistically significant DECREASE in conditional one-month VRP (Cheng's "low-premium response puzzle"); GARCH(1,1) forecasts are the most robust timing factor and exposure is cut ONLY in high-vol regimes (RV above the 80th pct ~20) (Yang 2024, Abstract + p.5-7 + tables p.38/45/47, english.phbs.pku.edu.cn/.../20240530092144610.pdf) [preprint]. **This is the closest published analogue to GSVIVS01.md §4 — the VRP-gap signal that goes flat before drawdowns.** Caveat: 1-month assets, per-asset (not common-window) Sharpe baselines; not a 0DTE study.

- **The seller's premium is rooted in jump/vol risk, confirmed from the buyer side.** Retail 0DTE buyers lost >$125M over 2021-2023 (avg $241k/day → $350k/day after daily expiries); returns rise with Gamma and Vega exposure (1-SD Gamma +0.019-0.045%, 1-SD Vega +0.018-0.094% over the day), and upfront-payment positions earn "significantly negative returns, which reflects the average additional compensation for the option seller" (Beckmeyer-Branger-Gayda 2023, p.3-4, wp.lancs.ac.uk/fofi2024/.../FoFI-2024-146-Leander-Gayda.pdf) [preprint]. Evidence the premium exists and is jump/vol-risk-driven — NOT a constructed short-variance drawdown study.

- **0DTE dealer hedging DAMPENS index vol — rebuts the "short-gamma is systemically destabilizing" narrative.** High open-interest gamma "does not propagate past volatility" and for >1-day options is associated with LOWER intraday realized vol; 0DTEs "do not destabilize the market" (Dim-Eraker-Vilkov 2024, Abstract + Intro p.2-3, westernfinance-portal.org/viewpaper?n=950096) [preprint]. The most recent in the lineage (Adams-Dim-Eraker-Fontaine-Ornthanalai-Vilkov, Oct 2025) reports ~60 annualized bps lower realized vol on 0DTE-present days via a market-maker hedging channel (SSRN 5641974, abstract-only; bps cited from QuantPedia's reading) [preprint]. Microstructure context for the chapter, not direct VRP-drawdown support.

- **The 0DTE pricing frontier confirms jumps/leverage/vol-of-vol dominate the short-tenor smile and ATM gamma is the mechanical tail source.** Closed-form skewness (leverage) and kurtosis (vol-of-vol) adjustments cut pricing RMSE to ~41% of Black-Scholes; the instantaneous volatility-process risk premium is highly predictable (R^2 21.2% univariate, 34.8% joint) vs 1.58% for the return premium; variance/volatility JUMPS contribute marginally to ultra-short-tenor VALUE; and 0DTE gamma "is very large, especially ATM" — the mechanical source of short-gamma/long-theta tail risk (Bandi-Fusari-Renò 2024, Table 4 p.43 + §7.2 + Intro p.4, portal.northernfinanceassociation.org/viewp.php?n=2240145012) [preprint]. **Caveat:** the 21.2%/34.8% predictability is for the instantaneous spot-VOLATILITY-process premium recovered from 0DTE options, NOT the realized-minus-strike variance-swap VRP a seller harvests — do not cite it as GSVIVS01's monetizable edge.

## Contradictions & Open Threads

1. **Is the 0DTE VRP actually monetizable, or only the upside/conditional slice?** Almeida-Freire-Hizmeri (2024) frame the 0DTE VRP as rich (up to 4× monthly) and the SSD-violation strategy as "highly profitable" (Sharpe 0.2-0.3 net); Vilkov (2024) frames the same-day VRP as "small and difficult to monetize after realistic frictions," monetizable only via conditional timing. These are not strictly contradictory (richness per-unit-time ≠ net-of-cost monetizability), but the chapter must not lean on the "4×" number as a harvestable edge. **Testable on our data:** backtest GSVIVS01 unconditional vs a VRP-gap-conditioned overlay (GSVIVS01.md §4 signal) over our SPX 0DTE sample; criterion = the conditional book's net Sharpe (deflated, Bailey-López de Prado) must dominate unconditional AND the QLIKE of the RV forecast driving the gap must improve over a no-skill `E[RV]=K_var` benchmark by a Diebold-Mariano-significant margin. If timing adds Sharpe but the RV-forecast QLIKE is flat, the edge is regime/seasonality, not forecast skill.

2. **Does a vol-forecast overlay's drawdown reduction survive at the 0DTE horizon?** Yang (2024) demonstrates Sharpe 1.54→1.76 and drawdown reduction, but on 1-MONTH variance swaps / VIX futures / straddles, monthly rebalancing — NOT 0DTE. The mechanism (vol spike ⇒ lower conditional VRP ⇒ sell less) is horizon-specific; the "low-premium response puzzle" is a one-month result. **Testable:** estimate the conditional next-DAY (not next-month) VRP response to a same-day vol shock on our data; if a same-day vol spike does NOT predict a lower next-day 0DTE VRP, the Yang overlay logic does not transfer and the GSVIVS01 signal needs a different conditioning variable (e.g. Bandi-Fusari-Renò's vol-of-vol / leverage term, which IS the predictable 0DTE object, R^2 21.2%).

3. **Continuous-variance strike vs jump-laden 0DTE realization — how big is the bias for OUR strip?** Du-Kapadia (2012) say the `1/K^2` strip mismeasures quadratic variation only when jumps exceed ~70% of variance, but that is the 0DTE regime; DDKZ Eq 42 and Carr-Lee Eq 9 say the short collects a positive cubic term on down-jumps. **Testable:** decompose our realized 0DTE quadratic variation into continuous (bipower) vs jump components and compute the realized third moment; if E[R^3]<0 dominates on drawdown days, the strike is structurally UNDERpriced relative to the seller's realized jump losses — quantify the bps gap between the booked strike and a jump-corrected (BKM/JTIX-adjusted) strike, and check whether the drawdown signal should condition on jump-share, not just the VRP level.

Also fixed misattributed citation in bottom-line section (HAR matching DL now cites Christensen et al. 2023 instead of Bucci 2020).

3. **Claim 86 (HAR "three coefficients"):** Line 783 said "three coefficients" but HAR has 4 parameters (3 coefficients + intercept), inconsistent with line 820 which correctly says 4. Fixed to "four parameters."

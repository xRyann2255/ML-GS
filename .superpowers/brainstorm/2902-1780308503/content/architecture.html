verify-diagram v2 — proposed architecture

One reusable engine. The skill runs it on one diagram; the workflow runs it on all of them.

Deliverable 1 · Skill (upgraded)

verify-diagram

Single-diagram verify-and-fix engine. Called by write-chapter after a diagram, or manually. Loops until the gates pass.

Deliverable 2 · Workflow

verify-all-diagrams

Discovers every TikZ figure across all three guides and fans out — running the same engine on each, in parallel.

Shared core: a Python/PyMuPDF helper (diag_inspect.py) — compiles, crops, and runs the deterministic checks. No LLM, fully unit-testable.

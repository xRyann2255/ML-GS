A systematic index that trades on dealer balance-sheet constraints

What it does

  • Rules-based strategy, rebalanced daily, fully mechanical
  • Turns risk-system outputs (VaR, factor concentration, scenario P&L) into cross-asset trading signals
  • Predicts: VIX innovations, asset-class drawdowns, momentum reversals, realized volatility
  • Trades through liquid instruments: VIX futures, variance swaps, asset-class futures, options

Dual value

As a product: Publishable index clients can buy exposure to through notes or swaps. Public-data version they can audit. GS runs the enhanced internal version.

As risk management: Time hedges and size exposures on positions the desk already holds. See the stress event coming 2-3 days earlier.

Feature engineering detail

FeatureRaw InputTransformations
VaR Utilization VaR usage, VaR limit Usage/limit %, rate of change, z-score over 21-day rolling window
Factor Concentration Factor-VaR by factor Herfindahl index (sum of squared shares), top-3 factor share, max factor share
VaR Dynamics Firm-level VaR, component VaR Delta VaR (day-over-day), 5d/21d momentum, rate of change
Scenario P&L Stress scenario results P&L rank, dispersion (std across scenarios), worst-case identity, skewness
Cross-Asset Flow Component VaR by asset class Share shifts (5d change in rates_share, equities_share), rolling cross-asset correlation
~15-25 total features. Each theory-motivated, not data-mined. Point-in-time stamped: VaR for date T is known at T+1 morning after the nightly risk run. Features use knowledge date, not application date.

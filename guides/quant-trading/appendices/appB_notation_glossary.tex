\chapter{Notation and Glossary}
\label{ch:app_notation}
\chaptermark{Notation and glossary}

This appendix is a reference, not a chapter to read. It has two halves.
The first is the \emph{master notation table}: every symbol used in the
book, what it means, and the chapter where it first appears. The second
is the \emph{trading glossary}: roughly a hundred and seventy terms in
alphabetical order, each with a one or two sentence definition and a
pointer back to the chapter that develops it properly. Entries here are
deliberately terse. Where they feel too brief, they are doing their
job: sending you back to the chapter where the concept is built up
properly.

\section{Master notation table}
\label{sec:app_notation_master}

All symbols below are declared once in \texttt{latex/conventions.tex} and
no chapter is permitted to redefine them. A handful of additional
symbols are introduced locally (for example, the Ornstein--Uhlenbeck
half-life $\tau$ in \cref{ch:mean_reversion_ou}, or the hazard rate
$h(t)$ in \cref{ch:credit_derivatives}). Those are collected here
alongside the global symbols so that this table serves as a single
reference.

\section{Trading glossary}
\label{sec:app_glossary}

Terms are arranged strictly alphabetically. Cross-references to other
glossary entries are in \emph{italics}. Where a term has multiple
meanings, the trading meaning is given first. Seeded from
\texttt{prosperity-wiki/trading-glossary.md} and extended from all body
chapters.

\begin{description}
\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}

\item[Adverse selection]
Losses a liquidity provider incurs because counterparties with superior
information trade against them. The core cost that widens bid--ask
spreads in the Glosten--Milgrom model. See \cref{ch:kyle_gm,ch:market_making}.

\item[Almgren--Chriss]
Optimal execution framework balancing expected trading cost against
variance of cost, yielding a characteristic exponentially-decaying
trajectory. See \cref{ch:almgren_chriss}.

\item[Amendment]
Modification of a resting order's price or size; many venues treat an
amendment to a more aggressive price as a new order for queue-priority
purposes. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Ask (offer)]
Price at which a seller is willing to sell. The \emph{best ask} is the
lowest such price currently resting on the book. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[At-the-money (ATM)]
An option whose strike equals (or is closest to) the current spot or
forward price. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[Augmented Dickey--Fuller (ADF) test]
Hypothesis test for a unit root in a time series. The null hypothesis
is that the series has a unit root (is non-stationary). Rejecting the
null at the chosen $p$-value is necessary (but not sufficient) for a
mean-reverting or cointegrated spread; the Engle--Granger test applies
ADF to regression residuals to test for cointegration.
See \cref{ch:mean_reversion_ou,ch:pairs_cointegration}.

\item[Authorised participant (AP)]
Large financial institution (typically a bank or broker-dealer) with the
contractual right to create and redeem ETF shares directly with the fund
in exchange for the underlying basket of securities. AP arbitrage between
the ETF market price and its net asset value keeps the two tethered.
See \cref{ch:basket_etf_arb}.

\item[Autocallable]
Structured equity product that redeems early if the underlying is above
a barrier on observation dates. See \cref{ch:equity_derivatives}.

\item[Avellaneda--Stoikov]
Stochastic-control market-making framework that derives the optimal
bid and ask quotes for a dealer managing inventory risk. The key
outputs are the \emph{reservation price} $r = s - q\gamma\sigma^2(T-t)$
(mid adjusted for inventory) and the symmetric quote distance
$\delta^* \approx \gamma\sigma^2(T-t) + \tfrac{2}{\gamma}\ln(1+\gamma/\kappa)$.
See \cref{ch:market_making}.

\item[Back-month]
A futures contract with a more distant expiry than the \emph{front
month}. See \cref{ch:commodities}.

\item[Backtest]
Simulation of a strategy on historical data. Must avoid look-ahead bias
and survivorship bias. See \cref{ch:backtesting}.

\item[Backwardation]
Futures curve state where near contracts trade above far contracts.
Typically signals scarcity or high convenience yield. See \cref{ch:commodities}.

\item[Basel III]
Post-2008 banking capital and liquidity framework (CET1, leverage
ratio, LCR, NSFR). See \cref{ch:regulatory}.

\item[Basis]
Difference between two related prices --- spot vs futures, cash bond vs
CDS, or near vs far contracts. See \cref{ch:commodities,ch:arbitrage_zoo}.

\item[Basis point (bp)]
One one-hundredth of a percent. $100\,\text{bp} = 1\%$. See \cref{ch:fixed_income_foundations}.

\item[Bid]
Price at which a buyer is willing to buy; the \emph{best bid} is the
highest resting buy quote. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Binomial tree]
Discrete-time lattice model for option pricing in which the underlying
moves up by factor $u = e^{\sigma\sqrt{\Delta t}}$ or down by $d = 1/u$
each step, with risk-neutral probability $p = (e^{r\Delta t}-d)/(u-d)$.
The recombining property ($ud=du$) gives $N+1$ terminal nodes after $N$
steps. Option prices follow by backward induction. The Cox--Ross--Rubinstein
(CRR) choice of $u$ recovers Black--Scholes as $\Delta t\to 0$.
See \cref{ch:numerical_methods}.

\item[Black--Scholes equation]
PDE $\partial_t V + \tfrac12\sigma^2 S^2 \partial_{SS}V + rS\partial_S V - rV = 0$
for European options under log-normal spot dynamics. See \cref{ch:black_scholes}.

\item[Book]
(i) The full set of resting orders for an instrument; (ii) the
portfolio a trading desk risk-manages (``running the book''). See \cref{ch:lob,ch:strats_role}.

\item[Brownian motion]
Continuous-time stochastic process with independent Gaussian
increments; the driver of most continuous-time pricing models. See \cref{ch:black_scholes}.

\item[Butterfly]
(i) Option spread: long two wings, short twice the body --- bets on low
realised movement around the body strike; (ii) FX vol quote convention,
measuring smile curvature. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs,ch:fx_derivatives}.

\item[Calendar spread]
Long one maturity, short another on the same underlying and strike.
Used to trade term structure of vol or of the forward curve. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs,ch:arbitrage_zoo}.

\item[Calmar ratio]
Annualised return divided by maximum drawdown. See \cref{ch:kelly_risk}.

\item[Call option]
Contract giving the holder the right (not obligation) to buy the
underlying at strike $K$ by expiry $T$. Payoff $(S_T - K)^+$. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[Cap / floor]
An interest-rate \emph{cap} is a portfolio of call options (caplets)
on a floating rate: each caplet pays $\max(\ell_t - K, 0)\cdot\text{notional}$
for period $t$. A \emph{floor} is the equivalent with puts. Caps are used
to hedge floating-rate borrowing; floors to hedge floating-rate lending.
Priced via Black's model on the forward rate.
See \cref{ch:rates_derivatives}.

\item[Carry]
Return from simply holding a position (funding rate, dividend,
convenience yield). ``Positive carry'' strategies earn yield while
waiting. See \cref{ch:fixed_income_foundations,ch:commodities}.

\item[Cash-and-carry arbitrage]
Long spot, short futures (or vice versa) to lock in the cost-of-carry
relationship. See \cref{ch:arbitrage_zoo}.

\item[CCAR]
Comprehensive Capital Analysis and Review --- the Federal Reserve's
annual stress test of large US banks. See \cref{ch:regulatory}.

\item[CCP (central counterparty)]
Clearing house that interposes itself between buyer and seller,
becoming the legal counterparty to both sides of a trade. Eliminates
bilateral counterparty risk via multilateral netting and daily
variation margin. Examples: LCH, CME Clearing, Eurex Clearing.
See \cref{ch:participants,ch:risk_production}.

\item[CDO (collateralised debt obligation)]
Structured security backed by a pool of debt instruments (loans,
corporate bonds, or CDS contracts). Cash flows are split into tranches
with different seniority: equity tranche absorbs first losses and earns
highest spread; senior tranche absorbs last losses and earns lowest
spread. Default correlation is the key risk driver.
See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives}.

\item[CDS (credit default swap)]
Bilateral contract where the buyer pays a running premium and receives
par-minus-recovery on a reference credit event. See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives}.

\item[CDX, iTraxx]
Standardised credit-default-swap indices for North America (CDX) and
Europe / Asia (iTraxx). See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives}.

\item[Charm]
Second-order Greek $\partial^2 V/\partial S\partial t$; rate at which
delta decays. Matters for overnight hedge resets. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Cointegration]
Property where two or more non-stationary series have a stationary
linear combination. Foundation of pairs trading. See \cref{ch:pairs_cointegration}.

\item[Contango]
Futures curve state where far contracts trade above near contracts ---
the ``normal'' shape for storable assets with positive cost of carry.
See \cref{ch:commodities}.

\item[Convenience yield]
Implicit benefit of holding physical commodity rather than a futures
contract (optionality of consumption, supply chain). See \cref{ch:commodities}.

\item[Convexity]
(i) Second derivative of bond price wrt yield; (ii) any second-order
effect that makes a P\&L curve smile up or down. See \cref{ch:fixed_income_foundations,ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Counterparty]
The other side of a trade. On a central exchange the CCP interposes
itself; OTC leaves bilateral counterparty risk. See \cref{ch:participants}.

\item[Covered interest parity (CIP)]
No-arbitrage condition linking the spot FX rate $S$, forward rate $F$,
and interest rates in two currencies: $F = S\cdot e^{(r_d - r_f)T}$.
Violations (CIP deviations) indicate funding stress, regulatory
constraints, or segmented capital markets. Forms the basis of FX
forward pricing. See \cref{ch:fx_derivatives,ch:arbitrage_zoo}.

\item[Crack spread]
The price difference between crude oil and its refined products
(gasoline, heating oil, diesel). Represents the refiner's gross
margin. A positive crack spread makes refining profitable. Traded
via futures spreads on crude and products; also called the \emph{3-2-1
crack} (3 barrels crude $\to$ 2 gasoline + 1 heating oil).
See \cref{ch:commodities}.

\item[CVA (credit valuation adjustment)]
Market-value adjustment to a derivative to reflect the counterparty's
default risk. See \cref{ch:risk_production}.

\item[Dark pool]
Non-displayed venue where orders are not visible pre-trade; used to
execute large orders with reduced market impact. See \cref{ch:participants}.

\item[Delta]
Sensitivity of option value to underlying price, $\partial V/\partial
S$. Approximate hedge ratio. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Delta-hedging]
Trading the underlying to keep portfolio delta at target (usually
zero); exposes the book to gamma and vega. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Depth]
Total resting quantity on one side of the book, or at a particular
price level. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Desk]
A trading team sharing a book and mandate (e.g., ``cash equities
desk'', ``rates vol desk''). See \cref{ch:strats_role}.

\item[Drawdown]
Peak-to-trough decline in equity curve. See \cref{ch:kelly_risk}.

\item[DV01 (BPV)]
Dollar value of a one-basis-point parallel shift in yields. See \cref{ch:fixed_income_foundations}.

\item[DVA (debit valuation adjustment)]
The mirror image of CVA: reflects the \emph{own-credit} effect in
OTC derivative valuation. If you default, your liability disappears,
so a higher own-credit spread \emph{increases} derivative value to you.
DVA is controversial because it creates a perverse incentive and is
difficult to hedge. See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives,ch:risk_production}.

\item[Engle--Granger]
Two-step cointegration test: estimate the cointegrating regression,
then ADF-test its residuals. See \cref{ch:pairs_cointegration}.

\item[Error correction model (ECM)]
Time-series representation of a cointegrated pair. Each variable's
change is driven partly by the previous period's deviation from the
long-run equilibrium (the \emph{error-correction term}). In pairs
trading, the ECM formalises how the spread is pulled back to its mean:
a large positive deviation implies a negative drift in the next period.
See \cref{ch:pairs_cointegration}.

\item[ETF]
Exchange-traded fund --- a basket instrument creating/redeeming in kind
with authorised participants, enabling basket-vs-constituent arbitrage.
See \cref{ch:basket_etf_arb}.

\item[Exchange]
Central venue matching buy and sell orders in a regulated, transparent
way. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Expected shortfall (ES, CVaR)]
Average loss conditional on being beyond VaR. Coherent risk measure. See \cref{ch:risk_production}.

\item[Fair value]
Best estimate of the true price of an instrument, usually blending
mid, microprice, and model inputs. See \cref{ch:price_value}.

\item[Feynman--Kac]
Theorem representing solutions of certain parabolic PDEs as expectations
of stochastic-process functionals; underpins risk-neutral pricing. See \cref{ch:black_scholes}.

\item[Fill]
The execution of all or part of an order. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[FOK (fill-or-kill)]
Order that must execute in full immediately or be cancelled entirely.
See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Forward]
OTC bilateral contract to transact at a fixed price on a future date. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[Forward rate]
Implied rate of borrowing/lending between two future dates, derived
from the zero curve. See \cref{ch:fixed_income_foundations}.

\item[FRTB]
Fundamental Review of the Trading Book; post-crisis market-risk capital
regime replacing VaR with expected shortfall. See \cref{ch:regulatory}.

\item[Funding leg]
In a CDS or structured trade, the leg that pays or receives the
funding cost. See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives,ch:risk_production}.

\item[Futures]
Exchange-traded, margined, standardised forward. Daily mark-to-market
is the key difference from a forward. See \cref{ch:commodities}.

\item[FVA (funding valuation adjustment)]
Adjustment for the funding cost of uncollateralised derivative
exposures. See \cref{ch:risk_production}.

\item[Gamma]
Second derivative of option value wrt underlying, $\partial^2
V/\partial S^2$. Long gamma profits from realised movement. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Gamma scalping]
Delta-hedging a long-gamma position to monetise realised vs implied
volatility differential. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Glosten--Milgrom]
Microstructure model where the spread arises purely from adverse
selection on informed traders. See \cref{ch:kyle_gm}.

\item[Greek]
Partial derivative of derivative value wrt a model input (delta, gamma,
vega, theta, rho, and higher-order). See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Half-life]
Time for an OU process (or AR(1)) to revert half-way to its mean,
$\log(2)/\theta$. See \cref{ch:mean_reversion_ou}.

\item[Hazard rate]
Instantaneous conditional default intensity, $h(t) = -S'(t)/S(t)$. See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives}.

\item[Hedge]
Offsetting position taken to neutralise a specific risk exposure. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[HJB (Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman)]
Continuous-time dynamic-programming PDE for optimal control; the tool
that produces Avellaneda--Stoikov and Almgren--Chriss closed forms. See \cref{ch:market_making,ch:almgren_chriss}.

\item[Iceberg order]
Resting order displaying only a small tip of the true quantity. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Implementation shortfall (IS)]
Difference between execution price and decision-time price, including
opportunity cost. See \cref{ch:almgren_chriss}.

\item[Implied volatility (IV)]
Volatility input that, put into Black--Scholes, matches the market
price. The market's implicit forecast plus a risk premium. See \cref{ch:black_scholes,ch:vol_surface}.

\item[Informed trader]
Market participant with a private signal about the fundamental value of
an asset. In the Kyle and Glosten--Milgrom models, informed order flow
is the source of adverse selection and drives bid--ask spread widening.
Contrast with \emph{noise trader}, who trades for liquidity or
portfolio reasons and provides cover for the informed.
See \cref{ch:kyle_gm}.

\item[In-the-money (ITM)]
Option with positive intrinsic value. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[Inventory]
Signed position carried by a market maker; drives reservation-price
skewing. See \cref{ch:market_making}.

\item[IOC (immediate-or-cancel)]
Order that executes whatever can fill immediately, the remainder
cancelled. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Information ratio (IR)]
Active return over tracking error of a portfolio vs its benchmark. See \cref{ch:kelly_risk}.

\item[Intrinsic value]
The immediate exercise payoff of an option: $(S-K)^+$ for calls, $(K-S)^+$
for puts. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[It\^o's lemma]
Chain rule for stochastic calculus, adding a second-order $\tfrac12
\sigma^2$ term. The workhorse for deriving SDE-based PDE prices. See \cref{ch:black_scholes}.

\item[Johansen test]
Multivariate cointegration test producing rank and cointegrating
vectors. See \cref{ch:pairs_cointegration}.

\item[KELP]
Prosperity 4 product; semi-mean-reverting with moderate volatility ---
classic market-making candidate. See \cref{ch:algo_rounds}.

\item[Kelly criterion]
Optimal log-utility bet-sizing rule $f^* = \mu/\sigma^2$ for normal
returns. Maximises long-run growth. See \cref{ch:kelly_risk}.

\item[KVA (capital valuation adjustment)]
Adjustment for the lifetime capital cost of a derivative. See \cref{ch:risk_production}.

\item[Kyle's lambda]
Linear price-impact coefficient in the Kyle (1985) model. See \cref{ch:kyle_gm}.

\item[Latency]
End-to-end delay from market event to trading response. Measured in
microseconds for competitive venues. See \cref{ch:participants}.

\item[Level (book level)]
Aggregated quantity at a single price in the order book. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[LGD (loss given default)]
Fraction of exposure lost on default, $1-R$. See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives}.

\item[LIBOR]
Legacy interbank offered rate, now replaced by risk-free rates (SOFR,
SONIA, \textsc{\'e}STR). See \cref{ch:regulatory,ch:rates_derivatives}.

\item[Limit order]
Resting order to buy/sell at a specified price or better. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Liquidity]
Ability to transact in size without moving the price much. See \cref{ch:lob,ch:market_making}.

\item[Long]
A position that profits when the price rises. See \cref{ch:participants}.

\item[Look-ahead bias]
Backtest flaw in which future information leaks into past decisions. See \cref{ch:backtesting}.

\item[MAGNIFICENT\_MACARONS]
Prosperity 4 product with imported variant, sunlight / sugar inputs and
a transport cost --- a locational-arbitrage product. See \cref{ch:algo_rounds}.

\item[Maker / taker]
Exchange fee structure in which limit-order providers (\emph{makers})
receive a rebate and market-order takers pay a fee. Inverted at some
venues (\emph{inverted markets}). Maker/taker economics drive order
routing decisions: brokers often route to venues that pay the highest
maker rebate, creating potential conflicts of interest.
See \cref{ch:lob,ch:market_making}.

\item[Mark-to-market (MTM)]
Valuation of a position at current market prices rather than historical
cost. Daily MTM drives realised P\&L, margin calls, and risk limits.
Futures are marked-to-market daily with cash variation margin exchanged.
See \cref{ch:participants,ch:risk_production}.

\item[Market impact]
Adverse price movement caused by one's own trading. Temporary impact
recovers; permanent impact does not. See \cref{ch:market_impact}.

\item[Market maker]
Participant continuously quoting two-sided prices and earning the
spread. See \cref{ch:market_making}.

\item[Market order]
Immediate execution order at the best available prices, walking the
book if necessary. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Matching engine]
Exchange software that pairs compatible buy and sell orders according
to the venue's priority rules. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Mean reversion]
Tendency of a time series to pull back toward a long-run mean. See \cref{ch:mean_reversion_ou}.

\item[MiFID II]
European regulation on market transparency, best execution, and pre-
and post-trade reporting. See \cref{ch:regulatory}.

\item[Microprice]
Size-weighted fair value, $\frac{Q^a \bidp + Q^b \askp}{Q^a + Q^b}$.
Tilts mid toward the thinner side of the book. See \cref{ch:price_value}.

\item[Momentum]
Tendency of recent winners to continue to outperform over some horizon.
See \cref{ch:momentum}.

\item[Moneyness]
How far an option is from the money. Usually expressed as $K/S$,
$\log(K/F)$, or in delta. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs,ch:vol_surface}.

\item[Monte Carlo simulation]
Numerical method that estimates option prices, risk measures, or path-dependent
payoffs by simulating many random paths of the underlying process and
averaging the discounted payoffs. Standard errors shrink as
$1/\sqrt{N_{\text{paths}}}$. Exact for path-dependent products
(barriers, Asian options) that resist closed-form solution.
See \cref{ch:numerical_methods}.

\item[NAV (net asset value)]
The fair value of an ETF's underlying basket per share, computed from
current constituent prices. The authorised participant arbitrage mechanism
keeps the ETF market price close to NAV: if the ETF trades at a premium,
the AP creates new shares to sell; if at a discount, it redeems shares.
See \cref{ch:basket_etf_arb}.

\item[No-arbitrage]
Assumption that no portfolio exists today that costs nothing and delivers
a non-negative payoff tomorrow with strictly positive probability. The
no-arbitrage condition is the foundation of derivative pricing: any
instrument whose cash flows can be replicated by a traded portfolio must
sell at the replication cost. Violations are called arbitrage opportunities.
See \cref{ch:price_value,ch:options_payoffs,ch:arbitrage_zoo}.

\item[Noise trader]
Market participant who trades for liquidity or non-informational reasons
(hedging, index rebalancing, portfolio adjustment). In Kyle and
Glosten--Milgrom models, noise-trader flow provides cover for informed
traders and is the source of market-making revenue. Contrast with
\emph{informed trader}. See \cref{ch:kyle_gm}.

\item[Notional]
Face amount of a contract; for derivatives, the reference amount not
exchanged at inception. See \cref{ch:fixed_income_foundations}.

\item[OIS (overnight index swap)]
Interest-rate swap in which one leg pays a fixed rate and the other leg
pays the compounded daily overnight rate (SOFR for USD, SONIA for GBP,
\textsc{\'e}STR for EUR). OIS discounting became the standard risk-free
discount curve after the 2008 crisis revealed LIBOR's credit risk.
CSA-collateralised derivatives are discounted at the OIS rate.
See \cref{ch:rates_derivatives,ch:risk_production}.

\item[Olivia]
Prosperity 4 bot trader whose trading-ID pattern gave away informed
flow in past editions. A ``trader-ID'' exploit example. See \cref{ch:algo_rounds,ch:top_team_lessons}.

\item[Order book (LOB)]
Collection of all resting buy and sell limit orders for an instrument.
See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Order-flow imbalance (OFI)]
Signed net change in resting quantity at the top of the book; a
short-horizon predictor. See \cref{ch:order_flow}.

\item[Ornstein--Uhlenbeck (OU)]
Continuous-time mean-reverting SDE: $dX_t = \theta(\mu-X_t)dt + \sigma
dW_t$. See \cref{ch:mean_reversion_ou}.

\item[OTC (over-the-counter)]
Bilateral trade negotiated directly between two parties outside a
central exchange. OTC derivatives (swaps, forwards, swaptions) are
customisable to any size, maturity, or structure but carry bilateral
counterparty credit risk and reduced pre-trade transparency. The CSA
and ISDA Master Agreement govern the legal relationship.
See \cref{ch:participants}.

\item[Out-of-the-money (OTM)]
Option with zero intrinsic value. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[Out-of-sample]
Data not used to fit the model; performance on it is the only honest
measure. See \cref{ch:backtesting}.

\item[PICNIC\_BASKET]
Prosperity 4 product constructed as a weighted bundle of underlying
items --- the canonical ETF-arbitrage example. See \cref{ch:basket_etf_arb,ch:algo_rounds}.

\item[PD (probability of default)]
Probability that a credit entity defaults over a horizon. See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives}.

\item[Price-time priority]
Matching rule: better price first; among equal prices, older order
first. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Put option]
Right to sell the underlying at strike by expiry. Payoff $(K-S_T)^+$.
See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[Put--call parity]
Identity $C - P = S - K e^{-rT}$ (no dividends). See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[POV (percent-of-volume)]
Execution algo that trades a target fraction of realised market volume.
See \cref{ch:almgren_chriss}.

\item[Prosperity3bt]
Community backtester for the Prosperity platform. See \cref{ch:prosperity_setup}.

\item[QR (quantitative researcher)]
Buy-side / prop research role focused on developing signals and models.
See \cref{ch:strats_role}.

\item[Quote]
A displayed resting price with its size; a market maker's current bid
or ask. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[RAINFOREST\_RESIN]
Prosperity 4 product with a very stable fair value around 10\,000;
textbook market-making target. See \cref{ch:algo_rounds}.

\item[Realised volatility]
Sample standard deviation of log returns over a window. See \cref{ch:vol_surface}.

\item[Recovery rate]
Fraction of notional recovered on default. See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives}.

\item[Reservation price]
Market maker's risk-adjusted mid, tilted by inventory. See \cref{ch:market_making}.

\item[RFR (risk-free rate)]
Overnight benchmark replacing LIBOR: SOFR (USD), SONIA (GBP), \textsc{\'e}STR
(EUR). See \cref{ch:regulatory,ch:rates_derivatives}.

\item[Rho]
Option's sensitivity to the risk-free rate, $\partial V/\partial r$. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Risk limit]
Hard bound on a risk metric (gross, net, VaR, greeks) enforced by
risk management. See \cref{ch:risk_production}.

\item[Roll]
Act of closing a near-expiry position and opening the equivalent in a
later expiry. See \cref{ch:commodities}.

\item[Roll's spread estimator]
Estimator of the effective spread from return autocovariance:
$s = 2\sqrt{-\mathrm{Cov}(r_t, r_{t-1})}$. See \cref{ch:spreads_roll}.

\item[SABR]
Stochastic-$\alpha\beta\rho$ volatility model; industry standard for
interest-rate smile fitting. See \cref{ch:vol_surface,ch:rates_derivatives}.

\item[Schwartz model]
One-factor mean-reverting log-price model for commodities. See \cref{ch:commodities}.

\item[SecDB]
Goldman Sachs' proprietary securities database; the canonical object
store for trades, prices, risks. See \cref{ch:secdb_slang}.

\item[Settlement]
Exchange-of-cash-and-asset step that completes a trade. See \cref{ch:participants}.

\item[Sharpe ratio]
Risk-adjusted return, $(\mu - r_f)/\sigma$, annualised. See \cref{ch:kelly_risk}.

\item[Short]
Position profiting from price declines; in equities, often requires
borrowing the asset. See \cref{ch:participants}.

\item[Skew]
Asymmetry of the implied-vol smile. Typically OTM puts trade above
OTM calls in equities. See \cref{ch:vol_surface}.

\item[Slang]
Goldman Sachs' internal domain-specific language used on top of SecDB.
See \cref{ch:secdb_slang}.

\item[Slippage]
Execution price minus expected price; combines impact, timing, and
discretisation losses. See \cref{ch:almgren_chriss}.

\item[SOFR]
Secured Overnight Financing Rate; the USD risk-free rate replacing
LIBOR. See \cref{ch:rates_derivatives,ch:regulatory}.

\item[Sortino ratio]
Sharpe variant using only downside deviation in the denominator. See \cref{ch:kelly_risk}.

\item[Spread]
Ask minus bid (book spread), or the quoted difference for a basket /
relative-value trade. See \cref{ch:lob,ch:spreads_roll}.

\item[Squid Ink]
Prosperity 3 product whose highly-mean-reverting signature made OU/z-score
strategies dominant. See \cref{ch:top_team_lessons}.

\item[Stat arb]
Statistical arbitrage --- systematic relative-value trading on
cross-sectional mean-reversion signals. See \cref{ch:pairs_cointegration}.

\item[Stop order]
Order activated when the market trades through a trigger price;
converts to a market or limit order. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Straddle]
Long call plus long put at the same strike; payoff $|S_T - K|$. Buys
volatility. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[Strangle]
Long OTM call plus long OTM put; cheaper than a straddle, needs bigger
move. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[Strat]
Goldman Sachs role combining quant modelling, software engineering,
and trader-adjacent work. See \cref{ch:strats_role,ch:secdb_slang}.

\item[Strike]
Price at which an option can be exercised. See \cref{ch:options_payoffs}.

\item[Survival probability]
Probability a credit has not defaulted by $t$: $S(t) =
\exp(-\int_0^t h(u)\,du)$. See \cref{ch:credit_derivatives}.

\item[Swap]
OTC contract to exchange cash flows. Most common: fixed vs floating
interest rate (IRS). See \cref{ch:rates_derivatives}.

\item[Swaption]
Option to enter a fixed/floating interest-rate swap at a specified
future date. A \emph{payer swaption} is the right to pay fixed (a call
on rates); a \emph{receiver swaption} is the right to receive fixed.
The vol surface for swaptions is indexed by option expiry and
underlying swap tenor. Priced via Black's or Bachelier's model.
See \cref{ch:rates_derivatives}.

\item[Theta]
Option's time-decay Greek, $\partial V/\partial t$. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Tick size]
Minimum price increment on a venue. Constrains spreads and quoting. See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Toxic flow]
Counterparty flow that systematically predicts short-term price moves
against you; adverse selection made concrete. See \cref{ch:order_flow}.

\item[Tracking error]
Standard deviation of portfolio return minus benchmark return. See \cref{ch:kelly_risk}.

\item[Trend-following]
Strategy family buying winners and selling losers at medium horizons.
See \cref{ch:momentum}.

\item[Triangular arbitrage]
FX arbitrage across three currency pairs when implied cross-rates
misalign. See \cref{ch:arbitrage_zoo}.

\item[TWAP]
Time-weighted average price; also an execution algo slicing uniformly
in time. See \cref{ch:almgren_chriss}.

\item[Uncrossed book]
Book state where best bid $<$ best ask (no immediate matching
possible). See \cref{ch:lob}.

\item[Value-at-Risk (VaR)]
Loss threshold exceeded with probability $1-\alpha$ over a horizon. See \cref{ch:risk_production}.

\item[Vanna]
Cross-Greek $\partial^2 V/\partial S\partial\sigma$. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[Variance swap]
OTC contract paying the difference between realised variance
$\hat{\sigma}^2_{\text{rv}}$ (measured daily over the swap's life)
and a fixed strike $K_{\text{var}}$, scaled by notional:
$\text{P\&L} = N_{\text{var}}\cdot(\hat\sigma^2_{\text{rv}} - K_{\text{var}})$.
Provides pure exposure to realised volatility, independent of delta.
Replication by a log-contract links fair variance to the implied vol
surface. See \cref{ch:equity_derivatives}.

\item[Vega]
Option's sensitivity to implied volatility, $\partial V/\partial
\sigma$. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[VOLCANIC\_ROCK\_VOUCHERS]
Prosperity 4 option-like product tied to an underlying rock price;
canonical volatility / Greeks case study. See \cref{ch:algo_rounds}.

\item[Volatility surface]
Implied-vol as a function of strike and maturity. Shape encodes skew,
term structure, and higher-order moments. See \cref{ch:vol_surface}.

\item[Volga]
Second vega: $\partial^2 V/\partial\sigma^2$. See \cref{ch:greeks_hedging}.

\item[VPIN]
Volume-synchronized probability of informed trading; a flow-toxicity
metric. See \cref{ch:order_flow}.

\item[VWAP]
Volume-weighted average price; both a benchmark and a common execution
algo. See \cref{ch:almgren_chriss}.

\item[Walk-forward]
Out-of-sample evaluation in which the model is re-estimated on an
expanding or rolling window. See \cref{ch:backtesting}.

\item[Wall mid]
Prosperity-specific fair-value construct using the deepest price levels
as more robust reference points. See \cref{ch:price_value}.

\item[Yield]
Discount rate implied by a bond's price. See \cref{ch:fixed_income_foundations}.

\item[Yield curve]
Plot of zero (or par) rates against maturity. Key shapes: normal,
inverted, flat, humped. See \cref{ch:fixed_income_foundations}.

\item[Z-score]
Standardised deviation $z = (x-\mu)/\sigma$. The workhorse signal for
pairs/mean-reversion strategies. See \cref{ch:pairs_cointegration}.

\item[Zero rate]
Continuously-compounded yield on a zero-coupon bond to maturity $T$. See \cref{ch:fixed_income_foundations}.

\end{description}

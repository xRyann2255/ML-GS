## Errors Fixed

1. **Claim 12/13 (line 203):** Coefficient values 0.36/0.28/0.28 were described as "estimates on S&P 500 5-minute RV (1990-2003)." Actually these are Corsi's simulation calibration values (p.182). Empirical S&P 500 estimates (Table 2, p.187) are 0.37/0.34/0.22. Data is tick-by-tick two-scale RV (not 5-min), sample is 1990-2007 (not 1990-2003). **Fixed:** Rewrote to distinguish calibration from empirical values and correct the sample period.
2. **Claim 16 (line 246):** Monthly sum was 32.70; correct sum of the 22 tabulated values is 33.70, giving mean 1.532 (not 1.486). **Fixed:** was: 32.70/22=1.486 -> now: 33.70/22=1.532
3. **Claims 17-18 (lines 251-257):** Cascading arithmetic error from Claim 16. Monthly term 0.28*1.486=0.416 should be 0.28*1.532=0.429; forecast 1.727 should be 1.740; daily vol 1.31% should be 1.32%. Also fixed the reference to monthly avg in the commentary (line 261). **Fixed:** was: 0.416, 1.727, 1.31% -> now: 0.429, 1.740, 1.32%
